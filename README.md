## Title of the Project
Real-Time Crash Detection and Response System Using Django and Computer Vision

## About
An accident is an unintentional and unexpected incident Speed is a major factor in auto accidents. If emergency personnel were notified of the tragedy and acted swiftly, many lives might be spared. Infrastructure and technology advancements made life easier for people. Considering the alarming increase in accidents that happen in India, this approach makes sure that the authorities are informed either in advance of an accident or right away.
Accident detection systems are one of the most useful pieces of technology for reducing the death rate from traffic accidents by treating victims as quickly as feasible. This article is based on computer vision, which is an emergency alert system developed in Python that detects the presence of an accident through object tracking methods. Saving precious human life requires an effective automatic accident detection system with automated accident site reporting to emergency personnel. This technology aims to detect incidents in advance and notify emergency personnel so they can provide quick medical attention to the injured party. Determining the accident's severity and alerting the rescue team in time are the goals of the study project.
